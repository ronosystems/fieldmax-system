Important Policy on Company Assets

As a staff member of FieldMax, you will be entrusted with company assets including but not limited to:

  • Electronic Devices: Laptops, tablets, smartphones, printers, scanners, and other electronic equipment
  • Inventory and Stock: Products, merchandise, and supplies
  • Cash and Financial Assets: Petty cash, sales revenue, M-Pesa accounts, and other financial instruments
  • Vehicles and Transportation Equipment: Company vehicles, motorcycles, bicycles, and fuel cards
  • Office Equipment and Furniture: Desks, chairs, filing cabinets, and office supplies
  • Security Equipment: CCTV systems, alarm systems, keys, access cards, and security passes
  • Intellectual Property: Software licenses, databases, customer lists, and proprietary information
  • Uniforms and Identification: Staff uniforms, ID badges, name tags, and safety gear

Liability and Responsibility:

  • You are personally responsible for all company assets assigned to you
  • Any lost, stolen, or damaged company assets must be reported immediately to your supervisor
  • A formal investigation will be conducted for any missing or damaged assets
  • If found negligent, you may be held financially liable for the replacement or repair cost
  • Repeated negligence may result in disciplinary action including termination

Asset Check-in/Check-out Procedures:

  • You must sign for all company assets issued to you
  • Assets must be returned in good working condition upon resignation or termination
  • Regular asset audits will be conducted
  • You are required to report any malfunctioning equipment immediately

Financial Liability Limits:

  • For willful damage or gross negligence: Full replacement cost
  • For ordinary negligence: Up to 50% of replacement cost or KSH 50,000, whichever is lower
  • For lost assets due to theft: You must provide a police report; liability determined by investigation
  • Repeated incidents may result in full liability regardless of amount
Consequences of Non-Compliance: Failure to comply with asset protection policies may result in disciplinary action including written warnings, suspension, financial penalties, or immediate termination of employment.
